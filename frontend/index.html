Natural language

Ask like a colleague, not a database

No more digging through charts tab by tab. Type a question the way you'd ask a colleague — "what's he on for his hypertension?" — and get an answer in seconds.

Traceable

Every answer cites its source

No answer is generated from thin air. Every response is grounded in your own documents and links straight back to the original record, with a confidence score showing how well-supported it is.

Access control

Isolation that's structural, not cosmetic

Roles are enforced at the data layer, not just hidden in the UI. Your organization's data is never visible to another account on the platform — by construction, not by configuration.

Compliance-ready

A full audit trail, from day one

Every query — who asked, what role, which patient, which documents were used — is logged and reviewable, with a usage dashboard built in for your administrators.
